# Privacy

CareCycle is software that runs on your device, not a service that stores your health data. It does not require an account and does not operate a cloud health database.

CareCycle stores your profile, period records, check-ins, trusted contacts, and settings locally on your device. It does not include analytics, advertising, trackers, telemetry, AI services, or a CareCycle backend.

Sharing occurs only after you choose a person, review an editable message, and choose an external composer. CareCycle can open WhatsApp, an SMS or email composer, or the system share sheet where supported; it cannot confirm delivery or retract data after it leaves your device.

Exports and backups may contain sensitive health information. Store them carefully. A person with access to an unlocked device, operating-system backups, screenshots, or an external messaging service may have access outside CareCycle's control.
